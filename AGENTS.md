## BuilderSpace project notes

- Design source of truth is the prototype in `BobLee-tech/builderspace-demo` (`index.html`, the "spec" view lists tokens, materials and the PRD → page mapping). Port visuals from there; keep the UI black / white / chrome with the mascots as the only colour.
- The five mascots (Vivi, Lumi, Geo, Momo, Kiko) are `public/mascots/*.svg`, generated by `scripts/generate-mascots.mjs`; edit the script and re-run it rather than hand-editing the SVGs. Their names, roles and colours live in `src/lib/members.ts`.
- All content reads go through `src/lib/data.ts` (`"use cache"` + `cacheTag`). Without Supabase env vars they fall back to `src/lib/sample-data.ts`; keep `<SampleNotice />` next to anything that can show sample data.
- Public content uses the anon key (`src/lib/supabase/client.ts`). Members sign in with a 6-digit email code; everything that reads or writes the session goes through `src/lib/auth.ts`, runs inside `<Suspense>` or a Server Action, and never inside `"use cache"`. `src/proxy.ts` only refreshes the session, so every write checks the member again. Without Supabase, development signs in with any code (a single dev cookie) and production refuses. RLS and the security-definer functions in `supabase/migrations/` are the security boundary. Schema changes go in a new migration file, never by editing an applied one.
- A member's card design (material, tint, pattern, tagline, up to 6 stickers) is a `member_cards` row; `src/lib/card-design.ts` mirrors its checks and the verified-only trigger (holo, titanium, `campus-` stickers), and sticker art lives in `src/lib/card-stickers.ts`. The card is drawn by `src/app/join/member-card.tsx` with `.mc-*` sized in `cqw`, so it reads the same at any width.
- Project cards, updates and comments are bilingual (`20261024000000_bilingual_content.sql`): the main columns are the Chinese side, `*_en` the English side that ops add by hand (`docs/translation.md`), and `original_lang` says which side the author wrote. Render the text with `<T zh={…} en={en ?? zh} />` (`projectText()` in `src/components/project-card.tsx` for project fields) and put `<TranslatedMark />` on the item.
- A project card shows only its one-line `pitch` (at most 36 by `displayLength` in `src/lib/text-length.ts`); the longer `problem` is the About section on the project page. Owners' cards reach visitors only through `project_owner_cards()`, which honours `profiles.card_on_projects` (on by default); `profiles` itself stays private. Visitors read `projects` column by column (`20261029010000_projects_column_grants.sql`): `owner_id` stays private (the site reads `owner_linked`), and a new column can't be read until a migration adds it to that grant. A member with no `member_cards` row, an owner on a project not tied to an account (`owner_id` null), and a `/people` team member without a card, get a card from `seededDesign()` in `src/lib/card-design.ts` (seeded by member number / owner name), so every project shows an owner card; the studio starts from the member's seeded design. Uploaded covers show in colour.
- Members' cards are public by default (`profiles.is_public`, a switch on `/me`): each at `/m/<handle>` (not indexed) and on the wall on `/people`, read only through `public_cards()` in `20261027020000_public_cards_and_email_change.sql`. Members change their sign-in email on `/me` with a code sent to the new address (`requestEmailChange` / `confirmEmailChange` in `src/lib/auth.ts`; Supabase has "Secure email change" off and `{{ .Token }}` in the Change Email template); `sync_member_email()` brings the school seal and zID with a school address and keeps them otherwise, mirrored by `afterEmailChange()` in `src/lib/members.ts`.
- A project whose `repo_url` is a `github.com/<owner>/<repo>` link gets its public repo stats (stars, language, last push, commits in 7 days) in `project_repos`, which pg_cron's `github_sync_run()` fills hourly through pg_net (`20261108000000_project_repos.sql`; optional `github_token` in Vault). Each run reads the last run's replies, so stats lag up to two hours. The project page shows them as one line under the hero (`src/app/projects/[slug]/repo-line.tsx`); a repo that is gone or private shows nothing.
- Only members (signed in, with a card) star projects, through `setStar` in `src/lib/auth.ts`. Who starred what is `project_stars`, private to each member; the public count is `projects.star_count`, kept by a trigger that doesn't touch `updated_at`.
- Owners get email about their projects (`20261101000000_owner_notify.sql`): comments, stars and review results go into `owner_outbox` by trigger and pg_cron's `owner_notify_run()` sends them batched through Resend; each project's settings are `project_notify_prefs`, set on the submit form and on `/me` (`getMyProjects` / `setProjectNotify` in `src/lib/auth.ts`, values in `src/lib/notify.ts`), and every email links to `/unsubscribe`. Resend's free plan (100 a day) is shared with the sign-in codes, so owner mail is capped; see `docs/email.md`.
- Member numbers are the real join order (`join_submissions.member_no`, backfilled by `created_at`); never renumber them.
- Forms submit through `submitWithoutReset` so server-side validation errors do not wipe the fields.
- Times are shown in `Australia/Sydney` via `src/lib/format.ts`.
- Event live pages: `builderspace.dev/live` (QR in `public/live-qr.svg`) opens the live event's `/live/<slug>`; the host switches steps from `/live/<slug>/host` on a phone with the `/admin` key, and the presenter's laptop projects the same `/live` page, which shows the results, the wall and the showcase on a wide screen. Steps and copy per event live in `src/lib/event-live.ts`, reads and writes in `src/lib/live-store.ts` (in memory in development without Supabase), the database side in `20261021000000_event_live.sql` (the `intent` step, asked after the guest's talk, in `20261021010000_event_live_intent.sql`). `builderspace.dev/intent` (QR in `public/intent-qr.svg`) opens that form on its own at `/live/<slug>/intent`, whatever step the host is on, while the live page is open. Pages poll every few seconds rather than using Realtime.
- The workshop prompt page `/prompt` is one static file, `public/prompt/index.html` (images in `public/prompt/img/`), served through a rewrite in `next.config.ts`; edit the file itself.
- Event results (recap, head-counts and the wall of people's sites under a past event's intro) are static: `src/lib/event-results.ts` plus screenshots in `public/events/<slug>/`, drawn by `src/app/events/[slug]/results.tsx`. Only list people who agreed to be shown, under the name they agreed to; `docs/event-results.md` is the checklist.
- An event can go up before its date is set: `events.date_tbc` (`20261106000000_november_events.sql`) keeps `starts_at` / `ends_at` as placeholders for sorting and the strip, while the site shows the page's `tbc` wording from `src/lib/event-pages.ts` (plain "待定" once the placeholder has passed, `eventTbc()`), with no countdown, calendar entry or search data. Once dated, update the times and switch `date_tbc` off. Event pages can also list `sections` (parts, cards, terms, flow, steps, a guest), drawn by `src/app/events/[slug]/sections.tsx`; an event that signs up elsewhere names the site in `signupVia`.
- Partners are static too: `src/lib/partners.ts` (logos in `public/partners/`) feeds the home Partners section after the plaza (an invite card with Kiko while there are none), "Supported by" on the event pages they helped with, and the footer's "Partner" link; `docs/partners.md` is the checklist. Partner logos are the one exception to "mascots are the only colour": they keep their own colours (the dark-background version) on dark glass, never recoloured. Only confirmed partners go in, never "in talks". UNSW and Arc are not partners and stay off the wall; the club is Arc-affiliated, so any Arc mark follows Arc's rules for clubs.
- Event materials (the take-home page `/learn/<slug>`, linked from Resources and the past event page) are static too: `src/lib/event-materials.ts` feeds a tool, not a file list (three paths, a kit that fills a GitHub username into links and prompts, a save-as-image poster drawn in the browser); `docs/event-materials.md` is the checklist. Copy on the site is short lines, never paragraphs.
- Mail goes through Resend on builderspace.dev: notify@ sends the sign-in codes and ops emails, hello@ is the club inbox (Resend Inboxes, apex MX → Resend); `/api/inbound-email` (webhook `email.received`, `src/lib/inbound-email.ts`) copies each email to the presidents in `HELLO_FORWARD_TO`, at most 15 a day. Addresses and DNS records are in `docs/email.md`.
